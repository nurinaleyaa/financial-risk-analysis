Financial Risk Glossary

Parametric VaR (Value at Risk)

Estimates the maximum potential loss of your portfolio over a given time horizon at a specific confidence level (e.g., 95%) assuming normal distribution.

Annualized Volatility

Measures how drastically the portfolio’s returns fluctuate over a year. Higher volatility indicates higher risk and price swings.

Sharpe Ratio

Measures risk-adjusted return. A higher Sharpe ratio means you are generating better returns relative to the amount of risk taken.

Risk Drag (%RC - Risk Contribution)

The percentage of total portfolio risk contributed by a specific asset. If %RC is higher than its Cash Weight %, that asset is punching above its weight in risk.
